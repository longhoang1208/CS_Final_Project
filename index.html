Career Groups

  • Investigative

    Description

    Work in this group revolves around research, analysis, and critical inquiry. Tasks focus on solving complex theoretical or scientific problems, conducting experiments, processing data, and developing new knowledge in structured or academic environments.

    Sample Occupations

    • Data Analyst & Data Scientist
    • Software Developer & Systems Architect
    • Medical Researcher & Biostatistician
    • Chemist & Physicist

  • Realistic

    Description

    Work in this group centers on concrete, hands-on tasks and physical output. Tasks focus on building, repairing, operating machinery, and managing physical infrastructure, prioritizing tangible results through direct interaction with tools, materials, equipment, or natural environments.

    Sample Occupations

    • Mechanical & Aerospace Engineer
    • Electrician & HVAC Technician
    • Commercial Pilot & Logistics Operator
    • Civil Engineer & Carpenter

  • Conventional

    Description

    Work in this group emphasizes organization, efficiency, and systematic data management. Tasks focus on maintaining accurate records, executing established protocols, processing financial or administrative transactions, and managing operational logistics within structured environments.

    Sample Occupations

    • Certified Public Accountant (CPA)
    • Financial Auditor & Actuary
    • Database Administrator
    • Compliance Officer & Logistics Coordinator

Software Developer

Job Description

Research, design, and develop computer and network software or specialized utility programs. Analyze user needs and develop software solutions, applying principles and techniques of computer science, engineering, and mathematical analysis. Update software or enhance existing software capabilities. May work with computer hardware engineers to integrate hardware and software systems, and develop specifications and performance requirements. May maintain databases within an application area, working individually or coordinating database development as part of a team.
