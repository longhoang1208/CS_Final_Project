Career Groups

  • Investigative

    People in Investigative careers are analytical, intellectual, and curious. They excel at observing, learning, analyzing, and solving complex problems, particularly in scientific, mathematical, or academic domains. Preferring working with data and ideas rather than people or heavy physical tasks, they enjoy working independently to understand how things work and uncover theoretical truths.

  • Realistic

    Realistic careers focus on practical, hands-on activities that involve physical interaction with tools, machinery, plants, or animals. People in this group prefer tangible, concrete outcomes over abstract theories or social discussions. They enjoy working outdoors or in technical environments where they can build, repair, or operate physical equipment.

  • Conventional

    Conventional careers focus on structured, orderly, and detail-oriented work involving data management, record-keeping, and process efficiency. People in this group thrive in organized environments where rules, clear guidelines, and systematic procedures are followed. They are skilled at maintaining accuracy, managing schedules, and working with numbers and administrative systems.
